### Preface

This is Volume I of a projected two-volume work on the mathematical structure of the Transformer architecture. The volume develops, in a single sustained argument, a geometric theory of self-attention, analyses the training dynamics of BERT as a concrete case study, and derives from these analyses a family of rationalised and incremental architectures that determine their own structure from the data.

#### Audience and prerequisites

The book is written for readers comfortable with linear algebra at the graduate level, the rudiments of differential geometry, and the language of modern deep learning. No prior exposure to the Transformer is assumed: Chapters 1–3 set up the architecture and the BERT case study from scratch.

#### How to read this book

Part I (Chapters 1–3) introduces the Transformer and BERT. Part II (Chapters 4–8) develops the local geometry of attention. Part III (Chapters 9–13) builds the motor formalism and the orbit theory of training dynamics. Part IV (Chapters 14–19) develops the rationalised and incremental architectures and reports the experimental validation.
