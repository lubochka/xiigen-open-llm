## 1. Why accuracy cannot be replaced with test coverage
A mutable learner has *operational deterministic invariants* (authorized data, typed output, schema/coordinate compatibility, journal apply-once, finite update, cold load, cancellation) and *empirical behavioral claims* (correct laws, better next choice, nonregression, speed/cost). Test **only the former narrowly when changed**; examine the latter with frozen novel families, paired runs, blind law-based grading and resource receipts. A passing compiler or Windows CI is necessary for some deliverables, but not a learner-quality metric. Do not delete genuine security or release checks.

Evidence labels have strict order:
`S0_SOURCE` primary facts and actual methods; `S1_USED` normal input→typed output; `S2_MUTATED` named θ/state before and after + once; `S3_CAUSAL` identical C0/T except θ, blind output improvement; `S4_COLD` persisted/reloaded adopted by ordinary user; `S5_INDEPENDENT` actual exact-candidate Astra invocation (never self-certified). Missing prior stage blocks the stronger *claim*, not other research lanes.

## 2. Dataset selection, DPO, and rank-pair separation
**Model roles (never substitute):** CODEX/GPT-6 Astra 6 UltraCode selects the legal learning case as delegated human; a separately authorized **external open Chinese model authors BOTH exact final** response-DPO `chosen` and `rejected` on the *same prompt x*; CODEX/GPT-6 Astra 6 UltraCode separately judges exact pair afterward, including `TIE`, `BOTH_BAD` and contradictory law. Trainer RP01 trains real Student/LoRA (compare Student and frozen reference response likelihood on exact complete UTF-8 bytes). Independent examiner is separate from pair judge. No unapproved provider/model/weights action implied by the plan.
**RP02 ranking preference differs:** A `(q,z+,z−)` pair must share query, domain-law-version, candidate *type*, eligible tenant/source/license/scope and admissible serving pool. `z−` may be semantically inferior but rankable under uncertainty; a deterministically prohibited foreign-tenant, wrong-role or unequivocally forbidden candidate is **excluded from the serving/ranking pool** and serves only as a hard-filter safety counterexample. Never bypass rights to manufacture hard negatives for the learned ranker. Do not call the ranker loss "Student DPO."
Each approved pair receipt must bind `pair_id, prompt_bytes_sha, chosen_bytes_sha, rejected_bytes_sha, author_model_and_license, selector_run, separate_judge_run, material_semantic_difference, domain/law_version, source_family, train/dev/SEALED_partition, provenance_rights, optimizer_recipient, parent_state_hash`; ensure edited/projected data retains the very difference that motivated preference. A rejected answer that is also valid → TIE, not forced teacher supervision. Deduplicate identities across restarts, not just within memory.

**GOOD response DPO (public C# illustration):** Question: what does `s?.Length ?? 0` mean? Chosen explains conditional access produces nullable result and coalescing supplies zero; rejected plausibly claims conditional access itself always produces 0. **WHY** meaningful same-x semantic defect and LoRA can be trained against the exact contrast. **BAD:** chosen from one question, rejected from another; one side authored by CODEX but labeled Chinese; rejected random gibberish. **WHY** violates causal preference, provenance or same-context objective. **EDGE:** compiler nullable/version unknown; chosen must state assumption, and a differently qualified correct answer cannot be blindly rejected.

**GOOD RP02 ranking example:** two LICENSED explanations for same question and type; one explicitly covers nullable context, the other is superficially similar but omits required context under ambiguous law → learned preference among admissible candidates. **BAD:** include a foreign-tenant record as `z−` for rerank learning and let θ trade off rights against similarity. **EDGE:** law confidently forbids output (hard veto) → no rank training from forbidden candidate; ties and empty candidates → abstain or deterministic fallback.

## 3. Three domain passports; same instrumentation, different laws
### D1: C# documentation / AST (start immediately)
- Versioned features: `language_version, nullable_context, AST_node_kind, receiver_type, result_type, operator_sequence, async/capture_context, UNKNOWN_mask`. Grammar/parser/compiler truth may be deterministic; prefer fit to task/role can be learned. Freeze **independent** source-project families and licensed references, not 8 paraphrases of one file.
- **GOOD:** `string? s = null; int n = s?.Length ?? 0;` ⇒ `0`; do not claim `s?.Length` alone is an `int` equal to 0. **BAD:** "?. and ?? are interchangeable" (wrong operator semantics). **EDGE:** `dynamic`, disabled nullable annotations, different compiler language version, overloaded members; qualify or return UNKNOWN.
- Grade actual normal answer: semantic law correctness, AST/type references, correct selected path vs lawful oracle alternatives, hallucinated method rate, abstention when evidence insufficient; source-family count N, not text-match alone. If compilation used as deterministic oracle, its one focused invocation is part of the *domain exam*, not a percentage-coverage suite.

### D2: EN→RU morphology, referents, syntax, negation (in parallel)
- Features: `source_language, target_language, POS_posteriors, dependency_edges, referent_id, antecedent_confidence, grammatical_gender/number/case, negation_scope, tense/aspect, placeholder_bindings, unicode_normalization, UNKNOWN_mask`. Keep several referent hypotheses when ambiguous: grammatical gender cannot be a global hard veto.
- **GOOD:** “Do not send until approved” → «Не отправляйте до одобрения» (negation and temporal condition retained). “Книга лежала на столе; он был тяжёлым” may lawfully use masculine `он` for `стол`, despite feminine `книга`. **BAD:** «Отправляйте до одобрения» drops negation, or `он` rejected solely because earlier noun is feminine. **EDGE:** quote boundaries, formal register, `ё/е`, mixed scripts, ambiguous antecedents; grade acceptable alternatives and uncertainty, not strict string equality.
- Grade negation/condition fidelity, role/reference accuracy on cases with true antecedent labels, grammatical agreement, discourse continuity, false hard veto, calibration/ABSTAIN, downstream answer correctness; compare p50/p95 and RAM.

### D3: SQL schema/dialect semantics (third generalization, not first-lesson gate)
- Features: `dialect_version, relation/column IDs, join_tree, nullability, ON_vs_WHERE, 3VL, cardinality, aggregation_scope, schema_permission, UNKNOWN_mask`. Hard source/tenant access is nonnegotiable.
- **GOOD:** `LEFT JOIN B ON B.a_id=A.id AND B.flag=1` may retain unmatched A rows. **BAD:** moving `B.flag=1` into `WHERE` and claiming equivalent under SQL NULLs. **EDGE:** empty B, duplicates, `IS NULL`, dialect-specific operators/unknown schema: use an allowed semantic oracle and qualify assumptions, not dataset memorization.
- Grade semantic equivalence across independent lawful database/schema families, null handling and privacy of tenant rows, final user query usefulness and cost.

C#/RU first make a two-domain pilot **after a real state update**; SQL is subsequent breadth check before multi-domain-generalization claim. Also preserve original domain-owner passports (orchestration dependency DAG, Angular versioned bindings, DTC responsive DOM/Figma assets, Builder provenance) for later RP slices; do not assert "all seven domains implemented" from these three.

## 4. First parameter learning and 6 nonserial experiments
Minimal domain-law kNN learner: represent `φ_d(history,candidate)` with meaningful typed features, provenance/rights filter `E_d` BEFORE scoring, source-proved hard semantic verdict `A_d`, and soft ambiguity under parsed hypotheses. Choose admissible query/candidate contrasts `Δψ=ψ(q,z+)−ψ(q,z−)`; optimize bounded `θ_d` with `L_rank=softplus(−θ_d·Δψ)+λ||θ_d||²/2`. Journal once, finite bound, checkpoint, cold load, rollback. Index retraining or constant manual change alone **not** this parameter update. Semantic law scores may be learned, but rights/source constraints may not.
Dynamic `k_d(q)` may return 0/1/many/ABSTAIN based on role entropy, referent uncertainty, domain shift, score margin, density, law-risk, p95+RAM. Compare *actual final user result* for k=0/1/3/learned policy, NOT a graph with different number of retrieved IDs while output remains first hit. If a policy is hand-coded it is adaptive-but-not-learned; name θ_k and its update to claim learned k.
Experiments independent, not six sequential bars:
- `C01` domain/intent/direction/stack OOD mapper vs frozen baseline; per-axis confusion and unknown/rejection;
- `C02` domain law φ vs old generic coordinate on reindexed same lawful corpus; downstream error and coordinate safety;
- `C03` **FIRST learned E2 test**: P original, C0 corrected code & no update, T identical C0 code/config/eligible corpus/candidates/decoder + one genuine θ update. `C0−P` engineering; only `T−C0` estimates learning;
- `C04` freeze θ, encoder, corpus & legal pool; change only fixed vs contextual k; measure path law errors/time/RAM;
- `C05` exact vs HNSW same eligible corpus & vector/metric; recall plus *final* correctness and rebuild/memory;
- `C06` typed one-neighbor vs lawful multi-neighbor fusion only if verified typed reducer. Else `NOT_APPLICABLE_TYPED_REDUCER`, not a blocker.
When RP01 Student is actually trained, factorial Student LoRA OFF/ON × kNN OFF/ON using frozen same exam and hard guards; compare interaction, independently of first lesson.

## 6. Minimal deterministic checks by CHANGED risk
Source-only/plan/exam/review → **0 new production tests**. Code edit with risk: choose minimal existing locally runnable test or one focused allow+reject pair for EACH actually new distinct boundary:
1. `KnnLogicalUnit.Train` normalized ID collision (“ A ” vs “A”) cannot point to stale/wrong payload. Existing check adequate ⇒ add none.
2. Tenant/source/license, law/version, wrong typed role, negative-only candidate and missing schema cannot become served output; strict allow+reject for changed admission only.
3. For a new θ optimizer: valid finite step AND NaN/Inf/overflow rejection, stable same-pair replay once, checkpoint restored on cold process; if each case already guarded, no new product test.
4. Cancellation/empty eligible corpus and incompatible encoder/index snapshot: caller returns safe fallback/abstention, no cross-version dereference.
5. Compilation (changed project only), narrow local functional call and direct diff review remain engineering sanity; native Windows security/release gate separately at delivery. Never invoke global Windows workflows or coverage percentage as a science acceptance gate.

## 8. Negative and warning retrieval memory versus rankable candidates (F primary check)
The original F/ARBITER-KNN-AUTHORITY-AMENDMENT-v1.md (blob `0408bddbcd595f2023dec5001b52bac68af7b8c5`, lines 49–124) calls prior failed examples **negative/warning neighbors**: keep them as *diagnostic memory* with source, verdict, rejection reason and which arbiter applies, **never serve their failed output as positive generation**. This is not the same as `z−` in a pairwise 02B1 rank comparison: that inferior z− must share same query/domain/rights/type and remain metadata-admissible; if a domain-law hard verdict forbids serving it, use as warning/arbiter counterexample outside candidate selection rather than a θ-score-ranked acceptable alternative. A ranker never overrides a hard source/tenant/legality/post-output arbiter verdict. A soft uncertain referent hypothesis is NOT an established hard-law failure; preserve multiple hypotheses and calibrated uncertainty. Examine warning-retrieval precision and dangerous false promotions on independent RU/C#/SQL families without adding broad product test coverage.
