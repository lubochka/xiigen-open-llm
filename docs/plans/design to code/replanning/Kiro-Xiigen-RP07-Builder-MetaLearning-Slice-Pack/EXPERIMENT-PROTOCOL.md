# RP07 preregistered scientific examination — useful teaching, not coverage

## Questions and falsifiers

H-S: a semantically lawful new Chinese-authored DPO pair improves *separate* Student on frozen unseen-domain family versus equal no-update and parent; falsified or inconclusive on no improvement/regression.
H-B: learned Builder B1 picks/constructs teaching opportunities whose downstream Students improve more in independent **round2** than frozen B0 with matched allocation, not simply getting a better pair-judge score.
H-G: graph-composed teaching route adds value over fixed-combination/random/ordinary controls on the same lawful subject and total resource budget; mere route diversity/graph topology is insufficient.
H-HYBRID: domain-sensitive deterministic legality+typed graph+learning gains a favorable quality/time/memory frontier versus constrained fixed, kNN-only, learned-selection-only arms; interactions cannot be attributed if multiple mechanisms change at once.

## Fixed original S07 scope — no convenient shrinkage

Five named comparison arms:
A = tested graph + trained/live Builder candidate strategy (only if trained and actual);
B = fixed-combination graph teaching control;
C = seeded random-route graph teaching control (freeze seeds/draws before observing results; keep bad draws);
D = ordinary admissible examples without graph pair structure, using their *real legal training modality* rather than a malformed DPO fake;
E = frozen-initial Builder B0, unchanged during round2, with comparable permitted candidate production.
Explicit builder/graph provenance stays true. For campaigns where approved Chinese external teacher must author BOTH final DPO sides, Builder and route arms **select or influence candidate construction**, not forge final model authorship. If the old S07 source requires a different named arm binding, reconcile original task and owner decision before effects; record exact source span and variant. Do not silently remove any control.

Two rounds:
- Round1: permitted Builder B0, identical Student parent per matched subject and arm, legal independent teaching material, Student utility/retention outcomes; if comparable evidence supports a lawful Builder meta-pair, train separate B1 and accept or retain B0.
- Round2: on **new source-family-disjoint** teaching allocations, compare cold accepted B1 (or explicitly retained B0) against unchanged B0 and all eligible controls; within each subject matched Students start at same preregistered parent. Old pair_id and old observed student lessons are not replayed on their lineage.

Six Student subjects = preserve **the actual six identities and per-subject axes from historical S07 materials**, not guessed labels or aggregate 28/6 substitutions. Discover full roster via original S07 program/tasks/owner-decision sources before freeze. Preserve translation directions, Angular body/relationship axes, SQL strata and uncovered fan-out groups if bound by source. **Seven domains in R21 curriculum are a different broader portfolio** and do not authorize inventing a seventh S07 subject. Additional seven-domain transfer studies are separate optional experiments with owner/budget.

## Operational metrics and validity

- Pair admission funnel: attempted/eligible/invalid, false-positive legal pairs, meaningful contrast, semantic chosen superiority, false rejected labels, TIE/BOTH_BAD/UNJUDGEABLE, source/rights/novelty and Chinese BOTH-side provenance.
- Mapping/law: domain classification macro-F1 + confusion/unknown/OOD, domain-law violation rate, source-stratum-weighted exact/correct quality, vector tokenization semantic consistency and migration compatibility.
- Dynamic algorithms: retrieval recall@k of lawful neighbors, multi-neighbor *used* proportion, route regret versus **enumerated lawful oracle paths**, typed association error, correct route/graph pattern transfer to unseen compositions. Exact versus ANN and fixed/adaptive k under identical candidate universe.
- Student lesson: paired held-out task utility per subject, change against C0 no-update, retention across previous families, warnings/negative safeguards, ordinary cold readback.
- Builder meta: improvement of **utility of lesson** produced by B1 vs B0, source-family grouped interval, success rate of novel lawful lessons, pair cost, trajectory from round1 to round2; distinguish B1 state influence from baseline selection and inter-arm study.
- Total resources: p50/p95/end-to-end wall time, teacher/judge/evaluator time, Builder and Student train time, PC peak RAM and disk, index/cache/reindex/persistence cost, cold load, all failed/cancelled branches and candidate fan-out. Honor original S07 budgets; do not invent thresholds.

Use confidence intervals/paired source-family bootstrap when n supports it, otherwise mark insufficient sample. Keep denominator, uncertainty and preregistered practical decision threshold; 95% in owner source is an **aspirational domain-grade target**, neither test coverage nor automatic early training gate. A small positive point estimate is not PROMOTE if uncertainty/cost/retention policy fails. Report per-domain and worst-domain behavior, ties, regression and sampling bias.
