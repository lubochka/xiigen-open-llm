## Применённый KH-COST recipe

[Данные и рубрика:68–104](experimets/cards/KH-COST-01.md:68): K1–K3 одинаковая AST-family сfor и array parameter, K4–K6 такая же no-loop family. Source proof: [Label22–72,FeatureVector76–106,Train122–125,Predict129–141](C:/Projects/llm_mvp_core/12.SemanticCompiler/Prediction/FileActionKnn.cs:22).
