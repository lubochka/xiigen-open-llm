## TU-002 — Guidance vocabulary is always gap

**Rule**: Mentoring, coaching, teaching, training, and onboarding verbs always produce gap regardless of context or object.

**Complete gap verb list for B34**:
mentor, coach, teach, train (human), guide (standalone verb), explain, demonstrate, show, clarify, instruct, advise, facilitate, sponsor, upskill, reskill, cross-train, onboard, shadow, observe, introduce (methodology), present (presentation), demo, help understand, hand off (knowledge), share (knowledge), review (mentoring context), assess (skills), evaluate (skills)
